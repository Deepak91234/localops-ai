PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. Part-time employees receive PTO proportionally to their scheduled hours. New hires accrue PTO monthly from their first day. Requests for more than five consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

[Page 2]
REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to three days each week with their manager's agreement. Client records must stay on approved company devices. Do not send client files to personal email addresses or public file-sharing services.

[Page 3]
EXPENSES AND SUPPORT
Approved business expenses should be submitted within 30 days with a receipt. Reimbursements over $250 require manager approval. Urgent support requests concern access outages; standard support requests are reviewed within one business day.

[Page 4]
HOLIDAYS AND LEAVE
Cedar & Coast observes 10 paid company holidays. Sick leave is available for illness and medical appointments; staff should notify their manager before their normal start time. This demo handbook does not specify parental leave or international travel allowances.
